Site Plan: Mate Culture – The Drink of Uruguay

Site Name

Mate Culture – The Drink of Uruguay

This name represents a site focused on the Uruguayan tradition of drinking mate, showing its history, preparation, and cultural importance.

Domain: mateculture.uy (hypothetical)

Site Purpose

The purpose of this site is to introduce visitors to mate, the traditional herbal drink of Uruguay. It explains how mate is prepared, its cultural significance, its health benefits, and the tools used (mate gourd, bombilla, thermos). The site uses friendly language and images so people new to mate can understand and appreciate the custom.

Scenarios (Audience Questions)

  • What is mate and how do you prepare it step by step?
  • Why is mate such an important part of daily life in Uruguay?
  • What are the health benefits of drinking mate?

Color Schema

The website will use the following colors, inspired by mate culture:

  • Yerba Green – #4E6C50 (primary)
  • Gourd Brown – #A47551 (secondary)
  • Bombilla Silver – #C0C0C0 (accent)
  • Warm Beige – #F5F0E1 (background)
  • Herbal Yellow – #E6B655 (highlight)
