Brew Guide

  1. Fill the gourd ~⅔ with yerba, cover, and shake to bring dust to the top.
  2. Tilt the yerba to create a slope. Add a little warm water to hydrate.
  3. Insert the bombilla at the lower side. Pour hot water (70–80 °C), not boiling.
  4. Drink and refill along the same side to preserve flavor longer.

Health Benefits

Yerba mate provides natural antioxidants (polyphenols) and gentle stimulants like caffeine and theobromine, which many people find give steady focus without a hard crash. It also contains small amounts of vitamins (B-group, C) and minerals (potassium, magnesium). If you’re sensitive to caffeine, start with a smaller serving and avoid drinking it close to bedtime.
